# For Engy 💌

A birthday website: a kraft envelope with a letter inside, a love message revealed word by word with our photos popping in, a 2-page photo gallery, and a cake to make a wish on. *Lovesong* plays in the background.

## Add photos → `photos/`
Upload them into the `photos` folder (any number). They show up automatically in the gallery **and** as little polaroids while scrolling.
Name them `01.jpg` … `24.jpg`. They appear in that order, 12 per page (6 per row on a laptop, 3 on a phone), each with its caption from `photoCaptions` at the top of `script.js`. Photos are resized and stripped of location data before upload.

## Change any text
Everything is at the top of `script.js` in `CONFIG`: her name, the letter, the love message, captions.

## Turn on the link
Repo **Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch → `main` / `(root)` → Save**.
